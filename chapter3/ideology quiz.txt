When did the Urdu --- hindi  controversy Start?
ANs: The Hindi-Urdu controversy arose in 1867 when the British government prepared to
 accept the demand of the Hindu communities of the United Provinces (now Uttar Pradesh) 
and Bihar to change the Perso-Arabic script of the official language to Devanagari and 
adopt Hindi as the second official language on demand of Hindi.

Q no.2 What is meant by separate Elections?
 ANS. Separate elections refer to the practice of conducting elections for different political offices 
or positions separately, rather than simultaneously or concurrently. This can happen at various 
levels of government, including local, regional, or national elections.

Q no.3 What is meant by Ideology of Pakistan?
  ANS. The "Ideology of Pakistan" refers to the set of principles and beliefs that 
underpin the creation and identity of Pakistan as a nation-state. It encompasses 
the philosophical, political, cultural, and religious foundations upon which the 
country was established. The concept of the Ideology of Pakistan emerged during the
 independence movement of British India and played a crucial role in the demand for 
a separate homeland for Muslims in South Asia, ultimately leading to the creation of Pakistan in 1947.
